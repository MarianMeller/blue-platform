Fictional case · DC-01

Payment missing from statement

Original creditor · Example Home Supply
Collection firm · Example Collections A

€123

Amount claimed in the 10 May statement

A receipt records a €50 transfer. The later statement still shows the full invoice and charges.

Document trail

01 May · Invoice
Household supplies, €100. Reference DC-01.
06 May · Transfer receipt
€50 sent with reference DC-01. Booking by the recipient is unconfirmed.
10 May · Collection statement

Question for the adviser

Where was the €50 transfer booked?

Bring the receipt and ask for a dated account ledger.

Fictional case · DC-02

Two payments, one reference

Original creditor · Example Mobile
Collection firm · Example Collections B

€76

Amount claimed in the 15 June statement

The statement credits one €30 payment. A second receipt for €30 carries a different reference.

Document trail

01 June · Invoice
Phone service, €120. Reference DC-02.
08 and 10 June · Receipts
€30 sent with DC-02 and €30 sent with DC-20. Both receipts name the same payee.
15 June · Collection statement
Principal €120 + fee €16 − payment €30 − merchant adjustment €30 = €76.

Question for the adviser

Which entry, if any, records the second transfer?

Bring both receipts and the merchant adjustment notice if available. Ask for booking references.

Fictional case · DC-03

Court document in the bundle

Original creditor · Example Repair Service
Collection firm · Example Collections C

€214

Amount claimed in the 04 July collection letter

The bundle contains a German court payment order (Mahnbescheid). The envelope and service date are missing.

Document trail

21 June · Invoice
Repair service, €180. Reference DC-03.
04 July · Collection letter
Principal €180 + fee €30 + interest €4 = €214.
12 July · Court document
Document heading reads Mahnbescheid. Its issue date is 12 July. No envelope is included.

Question for the adviser

When was the court document served?

Contact a qualified adviser promptly with the full court document and envelope. The issue date alone does not establish service.
