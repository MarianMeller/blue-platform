Initial application area

BLUE HEALTH · Germany-wide GKV

The proposed scope includes all German statutory health insurance funds, including AOK, Techniker Krankenkasse (TK), BARMER, DAK-Gesundheit, mkk and others. BLUE is not affiliated with or endorsed by these insurers.

1. Understand — make publicly available procedures and requirements easier to navigate.
2. Verify — connect explanations to official, current sources.
3. Improve — identify friction points and propose testable improvements.
4. Measure — assess whether a pilot actually helps people.

Early concept only. No personal health records are collected by this prototype. No medical or legal decisions are made.
